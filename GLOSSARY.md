# Mick's Micro Blog Theme

A bilingual personal blog on Micro.blog, where each post is sorted by what it's about and what language it's written in.

## Language

**Topic**:
A subject from the blog's fixed list, each with an emoji and an English and French label. A post can have several.
_Avoid_: Tag, sujet

**Lead topic**:
The post's topic that comes first in the fixed list, whose emoji represents the post.

**Post language**:
The language a post is written in, either English or French, declared by its Language category.
_Avoid_: Locale, translation

**Language category**:
One of the two Micro.blog categories, `English` and `French`, that declare a post's language rather than its subject.

**Unlabelled post**:
A post with no Language category, which includes the backlog. Its language is unknown, never assumed to be English.
_Avoid_: English post, default-language post
